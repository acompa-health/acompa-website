A different premise

Primary care cannot care for people it only sees episodically.

The shift is from a service people must find to a system that knows who it serves and remains responsible for what happens next.

Primary care today

  • Care begins when someone arrives.
  • Each visit is treated as an episode.
  • The patient carries information between services.
  • Missed care often disappears from view.

Primary care, redesigned

  • Care begins with everyone the system is responsible for.
  • The system reaches out, remembers, and follows through.
  • Information moves instead of patients whenever possible.
  • Every interaction strengthens the next.

The enabling system

The best available intelligence, carried into the work of care.

Acompa is building a platform that helps care organizations apply the best available AI across the tools they already use—without creating another competing record or surrendering clinical accountability.

01

Reach people in the language and channel available to them.

Patients, caregivers, and health workers can participate through the device, language, and level of connectivity available in the moment.

02

Bring the right context and intelligence to every care decision.

Relevant history, clinical guidance, and operating reality come together so each person can make the decision in front of them.

03

Keep responsibility visible until care is complete.

Care stays open across households, facilities, referrals, results, and follow-up until the expected work is done.

Voice across the system

Voice is not a product category. It is how patients can seek help in their own language from any phone; how community health workers can prepare and document with less typing; and how clinicians can spend less time reconstructing and recording history.
